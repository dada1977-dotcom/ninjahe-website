Case Study

Our client, a mid-size retail business, was struggling with disconnected systems. Their online store, inventory tracking, and payment processing were all handled by separate tools that didn't communicate with each other. This led to frequent errors, delayed orders, and a frustrating experience for both staff and customers.

NINJAHE LLC was brought in to design and build a unified e-commerce platform. The goal was to bring all sales channels, inventory data, and payment workflows into a single system that the internal team could manage without needing deep technical knowledge.

What we delivered

We started with a thorough discovery phase, mapping out every step of the client's order fulfillment process. From there, we built a custom web application that integrated directly with their existing suppliers and shipping providers.

The new platform included automated inventory syncing, a streamlined checkout flow, and a reporting dashboard that gave the management team real-time visibility into sales performance.

  • 1
    Unified Order Management

    All orders from the website, phone, and in-store now flow into a single dashboard. Staff can process, track, and update orders without switching between systems.

  • 2
    Real-Time Inventory Sync

    Stock levels update automatically across all sales channels. This eliminated overselling and reduced the time staff spent on manual inventory counts.

  • 3
    Integrated Payment Processing

    We connected the platform to the client's preferred payment gateway, supporting credit cards, digital wallets, and purchase orders in one checkout flow.

After launch, we provided a two-week training period for the client's staff. We also delivered written documentation and recorded walkthrough videos so the team could reference them later.

Results after six months

Order processing time dropped by roughly 40%. Inventory discrepancies went from a weekly problem to a rare exception. The client reported that their team could handle a higher volume of orders without adding headcount.

The platform has been running in production for over a year with minimal downtime. We continue to provide maintenance and small feature updates on a monthly retainer basis.
