Case Study

Our client, a growing e-commerce company, was tracking sales, customer activity, and operational metrics across a patchwork of spreadsheets and disconnected tools. Managers spent hours each week exporting data by hand, and numbers from different sources rarely matched.

NINJAHE LLC was engaged to design and build a custom SaaS dashboard that would pull data from all of the client's systems into one clear, real-time view, with reports that different teams could tailor to their own needs.

What we delivered

We began by auditing every data source the client relied on: their online store, payment processor, shipping platform, and CRM. We then built a web-based dashboard with secure API connections to each system, so data flows in automatically and stays current.

The dashboard included a custom report builder, role-based access so each team sees only what it needs, and a mobile-friendly interface for managers checking numbers on the go.

  • 1
    Real-Time Data Aggregation

    Sales, customer, and operations data from all connected systems syncs into the dashboard continuously, eliminating manual exports and end-of-week reconciliation.

  • 2
    Custom Report Builder

    Teams can build, save, and schedule their own reports with drag-and-drop filters and time ranges, without waiting for a developer to write queries.

  • 3
    Role-Based Access Control

    Executives, sales staff, and operations teams each get a view matched to their responsibilities, keeping sensitive figures limited to the people who need them.

After launch, we ran hands-on training sessions with each team and provided written documentation plus short reference videos, so staff could adopt the new workflow without disrupting daily operations.

Results after six months

Weekly reporting time dropped from several hours to minutes. Data discrepancies between systems effectively disappeared, and management now reviews a single shared set of numbers in every meeting.

The dashboard has been running in production for over a year. We continue to maintain the integrations and ship small improvements on a monthly retainer basis.
